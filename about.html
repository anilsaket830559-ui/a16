About Roastly

A small site about cooking dinner for other people without losing the evening.

Roastly came out of a notebook kept beside the oven: what went in when, what came out dry, and which Sunday the vegetables went cold because the gravy took longer than expected. The tools here are that notebook, tidied up.

What we publish

Two long guides and three free tools. The Roasting Guide covers temperatures, timing, resting and gravy. The Hosting Guide covers menus, making ahead and pacing an evening. The tools answer the three questions that came up most while we were writing them.

How we stay independent

We take no payment, ingredients or equipment in exchange for mentioning a brand, and our recipes and timings are deliberately brand-neutral. There are no shopping links here. If ads appear, a third party serves them and they have no influence on what we write.

How we test

Everything is cooked in an ordinary home kitchen with one oven and four hob rings. Where a result depends on your oven, your tray or the shape of the joint, we say so rather than pretending there is one right number. Our Editorial Policy explains how we research, review and correct what we publish.

Food safety and allergies

Cooking times here are guides. Always confirm poultry and minced meat are cooked through with a thermometer. Recipes and advice may involve common allergens including wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts and fish, so read labels and adapt to your own needs. See our Disclaimer.

Corrections

If a timing fails in your oven or a tool gives an odd answer, write to hello@roastly.com or use the contact page. Corrections go to the front of the queue.
